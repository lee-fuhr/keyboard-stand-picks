How we choose our picks

We don’t run lab tests or time trials, we’re an independent research outfit. Our picks come from combing through long-term owner reviews, talking to rental shops and keyboard technicians, and reading published reviews from reputable music gear outlets. We look for stands that have been in production for years and still hold up after daily use. The things that actually break on keyboard stands are almost never the steel tubing, it’s the plastic locking clips, cheap rubber feet, and wimpy hinge pins. So we prioritize models with all-metal adjustment mechanisms, thick nonslip feet, and a proven track record for staying tight over years of folding and unfolding. Stability under a heavy keyboard at full extension is the non-negotiable filter.
